Piano sound (piano.wav)
-----------------------
Salamander Grand Piano V3 by Alexander Holm (Yamaha C5 grand piano),
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The mp3 version used here is the one hosted by the Tone.js project:
https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/

piano.wav was assembled from those recordings by tools/build-piano.ps1
(keys C1-C7, 8 seconds per key; keys between recordings are pitch-shifted
by one semitone).

Bass sound (bass.wav, web/bass-samples.js)
------------------------------------------
Pop styles: electric bass samples from tonejs-instruments by Nicholaus
Brosowsky, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0):
https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments
bass.wav was assembled from those recordings by tools/build-bass.ps1
(keys C#1-E4, 6 seconds per key).

Double bass (upright.wav, web/upright-samples.js)
-------------------------------------------------
Jazz styles: pizzicato double bass from VSCO 2 Community Edition by
Versilian Studios, released under CC0 (public domain):
https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE (Strings/Solo Contrabass/Pizz)
upright.wav was assembled from those recordings by tools/build-upright.ps1
(keys C#1-E4, 6 seconds per key).

Drum sounds (drums.wav, web/drum-samples.js)
--------------------------------------------
One-shots from VCSL, the Versilian Community Sample Library,
released under CC0 (public domain):
https://github.com/michaelwillis/VCSL

drums.wav was assembled from those recordings by tools/build-drums.ps1
(drum notes 36-51, 3 seconds per note).
